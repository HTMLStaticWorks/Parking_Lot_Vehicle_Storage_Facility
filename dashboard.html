Frequently Asked Questions

How does license plate gate scan access function?

Our ANPR cameras detect your license plate 10 meters before the gate entry barrier. If matched to a current booking, the terminal displays a welcome message and opens the boom automatically.

What happens if my flight is delayed and I overstay?

No penalty fines are charged. Our platform matches flight number updates in real time. If overstay occurs, the exit reader automatically computes the extra duration at standard rates billed to your card.

How do I retrieve my stored car from the vault?

Simply click "Request Checkout" inside your storage panel at least 12 hours prior to collection. Our detailing staff will wake the vehicle, adjust cold tire pressures, perform detail wash, and align it at the collection zone.
