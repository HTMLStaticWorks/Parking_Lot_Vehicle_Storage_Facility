Company Story

The Pioneers of Smart Parking

We build physical and digital systems allowing commercial properties, airports, and private vehicle collectors to manage spaces smoothly.

Establishing Next-Generation Vehicle Infrastructure

Founded in 2018, ParkShield was created to address a simple issue: the severe lack of efficiency and security in airport and commercial parking networks. What began as a single pilot installation of camera loops has grown into a connected network managing over 12,000 slots across multiple premium destinations.

We build robust concrete structures and integrate them with real-time web databases, automatic plate recognition (ANPR), smart level-indicator lights, and active climate control vaults.

Our Mission

To connect physical vehicle facilities to digital wallets, making driving and storing touchless and secure.

Our Vision

Creating a global network of self-operating structures powered by solar grids and smart chargers.

Our Operational Standards

Each facility operates under tight SLA structures to ensure consistent security, cleanliness, and device availability.

99.9% Uptime

ANPR cameras, gate boom barriers, and ticketing terminals are kept online 24/7 via power backups.

Carbon Offsetting

Roofs are equipped with high-yield solar arrays to generate clean power for parking decks and EV charge units.

Customer Care

Automated booking assistants and terminal helpline intercom systems resolve user issues within 60 seconds.
