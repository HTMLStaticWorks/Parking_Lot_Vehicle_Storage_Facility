Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding reservations, access passes, billing, and security.

How do I access the parking lot after booking?

Once you reserve a space, you will receive a digital QR pass in your email and client dashboard. Our gates are also equipped with ANPR license readers that will match your license plate and open automatically upon arrival.

Can I extend my parking reservation while away?

Yes, you can extend your stay at any time directly through your dashboard. Simply locate your active pass and select the duration extension option.

What is included in the Climate-Controlled Storage Vault service?

Premium vaults feature continuous climate-controlled airflow, bi-weekly tire checks, automatic connection to smart trickle battery chargers, and premium dust covers.

Is there electric vehicle (EV) charging available?

Yes, we have dedicated EV charging bays equipped with Level 2 and Level 3 DC fast chargers. You can add charger reservation options when booking your parking space.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

You can cancel any reservation up to 24 hours before your scheduled arrival time for a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours of arrival are subject to a nominal fee.
